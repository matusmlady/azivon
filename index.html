Azivon

 Welcome to our website! Azivon is a strategy boardgame where you need expand your territory and conquer your opponents. All of this with a sheet of paper and a pencil. This website is a place to find the latest rules and it provides you the ability to generate a map to play on. After generating you can print it and you are ready to go!

Latest updates

  • Fortifications now spawn on the map same as castles!
  • You can now mine mountains for stones to build castles!

Setup

  • Print the map and get a pencil.
  • Place your spawns evenly and randomly (turn the page upside down).
  • If there are more players than corners, choose spawns equidistantly.

Expansion

  • Each turn a player receives 2 actions.
  • They then expand by taking tiles.
  • Expanding to 1 empty tile costs you 1 action.
  • You can expand at maximum 2 tiles away from your territory.
  • You don't move, you expand.
